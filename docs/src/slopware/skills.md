# Skills

The [skills] extends your agent capabilities.
Simply place or symlink them into your [skills folder].

```sh
crush
├── crush.json
└── skills
    └── <skill>
        ├── references
        │   ├── <reference1>
        │   └── <reference2>
        └── SKILL.md
```

> The [carapace skill] is the important one, most of the others are simply byproducts.
